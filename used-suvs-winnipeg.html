Choose the SUV size you actually need

Compact SUVs can be easier to park and may use less fuel. Midsize and three-row SUVs can add passenger and cargo room but may cost more to fuel, insure and maintain. Compare interior space and seating with your regular passengers and cargo in mind.

AWD is useful—but tires still matter

All-wheel drive can help with acceleration on slippery roads. It does not replace good winter tires for braking and steering. Review tire condition, drivetrain warnings and how the SUV behaves during a proper test drive.

Inspect the expensive systems

Check transmission behaviour, suspension noise, steering, climate control, electronics, fluid leaks and underbody corrosion. Review the VIN and available history information, and consider an independent pre-purchase inspection.
