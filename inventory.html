Frequently asked questions

Can I book a test drive without selecting a vehicle?

Yes. You can reserve an available time first and discuss suitable vehicles when the M.O. Motors team follows up.

Are prices and availability updated online?

The inventory displays the latest information available from our dealership database. Vehicles can sell or change status, so contact us to confirm availability before travelling.

Can I trade in or sell my current vehicle?

Yes. Use the online trade-in form to tell us whether you want to trade, sell or consign your vehicle.

What if I cannot find the make or model I want?

Submit a vehicle sourcing request with your preferred makes, models, budget, mileage and features.
