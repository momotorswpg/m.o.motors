01

Powertrain protection

Focused coverage for the components that keep your vehicle moving.

ENTRY LEVEL

Essential Powertrain

Flexible protection for core engine, transmission, differential and transfer-case components.

  • Core powertrain components
  • Seals and gaskets for covered parts
  • Roadside and travel assistance benefits
  • Technology and deductible upgrades may be available
Best forOlder or higher-mileage vehicles needing affordable core protection.
ENHANCED

Enhanced Powertrain

Higher-level powertrain protection with more flexibility to add mechanical and convenience coverage.

  • Engine, transmission and driveline systems
  • Seals and gaskets for covered parts
  • Optional A/C, electrical and technology coverage
  • Higher claim-limit options may be available
Best forDrivers prioritizing major repair protection with customizable upgrades.
02

Mechanical coverage

Broader choices for everyday systems, comfort equipment and technology.

EVERYDAY VALUE

Everyday Mechanical

Powertrain protection plus several commonly repaired comfort and electrical components.

  • Engine, transmission and drivetrain
  • Air conditioning
  • Alternator, starter and water pump
  • Optional technology coverage may be available
Best forBroad everyday protection on vehicles of many ages and mileages.
FLEXIBLE

Flexible Comprehensive

A wider mechanical plan designed to cover more systems while remaining adaptable to vehicle eligibility.

  • Powertrain, A/C and electrical systems
  • Brakes, steering and front suspension
  • Supplementary mechanical parts
  • Optional technology and zero-deductible choices
Best forCustomers seeking broad coverage for an older or higher-mileage vehicle.
PREMIER

Premier Comprehensive

Our broadest traditional-vehicle option, covering major mechanical systems and a wider range of electronics.

  • Comprehensive mechanical and powertrain coverage
  • Cooling, fuel, brakes, steering and suspension
  • Advanced technology options
  • Hybrid/electric and factory top-up options may be available
Best forEligible newer vehicles where maximum breadth is the priority.
03

Layered protection

Broader protection after purchase, followed by long-term powertrain coverage.

TWO-STAGE COVERAGE

Complete Start + Long-Term Powertrain

Begins with comprehensive mechanical coverage during the initial ownership period, then continues with core powertrain protection for the remainder of the selected term.

  • Initial A/C, brakes, electrical, steering and mechanical coverage
  • Long-term engine, transmission, differential and transfer-case coverage
  • Roadside and travel assistance benefits
  • Optional technology and deductible upgrades may be available
Best forCustomers who want extra confidence immediately after purchase and essential protection afterward.
04

Mileage-limit protection

Coverage continues until the vehicle reaches the selected odometer limit instead of expiring after a set number of months.

NO SET TIME LIMIT

MileageGuard

Mechanical and powertrain coverage that continues until the selected odometer reading is reached.

  • Powertrain, A/C and selected electrical systems
  • Cooling, fuel, seals and gaskets
  • Optional technology and added mechanical coverage
  • Multiple final odometer limits may be available
Best forLower annual-mileage drivers who prefer an odometer-based finish line.
PREMIER MILEAGE COVERAGE

MileageGuard Premier

Broad comprehensive protection with no set time expiry, continuing to the selected odometer limit.

  • Extensive mechanical, safety and technology systems
  • Powertrain, climate, fuel, brakes and suspension
  • Roadside, travel and rental benefits
  • Hybrid/electric components may be available
Best forEligible newer vehicles needing broad coverage without a calendar deadline.
05

Electric vehicle protection

Dedicated coverage for EV drive systems, high-voltage components and vehicle technology.

EV CORE

EV Essential

Purpose-built protection for essential electric-drive components and key mechanical systems.

  • Electric drive unit and gearbox
  • Climate control, brakes, driveline and steering
  • High-voltage battery and technology options may be available
  • Roadside and travel assistance benefits
Best forEligible EVs needing dependable core protection with flexible upgrades.
EV PREMIER

EV Premier

High-level EV coverage combining battery, electric-drive, chassis, climate and advanced technology protection.

  • High-voltage battery and battery management
  • Electric drive unit, gearbox and related electronics
  • Comprehensive steering, suspension, brakes and climate systems
  • Advanced driver-assistance and technology components
Best forEligible newer EVs where broad protection and higher limits matter most.
