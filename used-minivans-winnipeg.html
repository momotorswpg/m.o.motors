Check the features your passengers use

Operate every sliding door, seat latch, window, climate-control zone and power feature. Confirm that child-seat anchors, third-row access and cargo arrangements work for your household or business.

Inspect common wear areas

Look at door tracks, interior trim, seat mechanisms, tires, brakes and suspension. Test the heater and defroster for Winnipeg winters, watch for warning lights and review available history information.

Compare space and operating cost

A minivan can offer more usable passenger and cargo space than many SUVs, but fuel, tire and maintenance costs still vary by model. Compare the vehicle’s real condition and total ownership cost—not only the number of seats.
