Compare condition before model year

The newest vehicle is not automatically the best value. Maintenance history, corrosion, warning lights, tire condition, brakes, suspension and how the vehicle drives can matter more than the year alone. Ask for the VIN, review available history information and consider an independent pre-purchase inspection.

Plan for Winnipeg driving

Cold weather and rough roads can expose weak batteries, worn suspension components and poor tires. Check the heater and defroster, cold-start behaviour, tire tread and whether the vehicle tracks and brakes properly. Good winter tires can be more important than choosing all-wheel drive for many city drivers.

Budget for more than the sticker price

Allow room for taxes, registration, insurance, fuel, seasonal tires and upcoming maintenance. When financing is available, compare the selling price, disclosed fees, down payment, interest rate, term and total cost of borrowing—not only the scheduled payment.
