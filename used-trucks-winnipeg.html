Match the truck to the job

Daily commuting, hauling tools, towing and family use can require different cab, box, engine and drivetrain combinations. Confirm the exact payload and towing specifications for the specific truck, configuration and equipment.

Check for evidence of hard use

Inspect the box, hitch, frame, suspension, tires, brakes and underbody. Look for corrosion, leaks, warning lights and uneven tire wear. Test four-wheel drive where it is safe and appropriate, and review available history information.

Budget for total truck ownership

Fuel, tires, insurance, maintenance and repairs can cost more than on a smaller vehicle. Compare the selling price and operating costs together, especially if the truck will mainly be used for commuting.
