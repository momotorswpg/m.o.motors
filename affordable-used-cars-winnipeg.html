Affordable should still be carefully evaluated

At lower price points, age and kilometres can vary widely. Compare maintenance history, body and undercarriage condition, tires, brakes, warning lights and how the vehicle behaves on a proper test drive. Consider an independent inspection whenever you want another assessment.

Keep room in the budget

Plan for registration, insurance, fuel, seasonal tires and upcoming maintenance. If you are financing, compare the selling price, down payment, trade-in, interest rate, term and fees together. A lower scheduled payment does not necessarily mean a lower total cost.
